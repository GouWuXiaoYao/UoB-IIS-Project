# Figure captions

Paste-ready captions. Everything below is computed from the logs under
`runs/`; the numbers match `results.xlsx`.

**fig1_outcome_bars.png**

Figure 1. Mean debt change per condition, whiskers are 95% confidence intervals of the mean (Student t). Sessions per condition: C0 n = 6, C1 n = 6, paired by task. Every paired difference is exactly zero, so no interval for the difference exists; this is a property of the manipulation (the suggestion is identical in both arms), not evidence that the conditions are equivalent in general.

**fig2_paired_slope.png**

Figure 2. Debt change for each task, paired by task (baseline left, governance right). 6 of 6 pairs are exactly equal.

**fig3_events_per_session.png**

Figure 3. Mean number of logged events per session: baseline 11.0, governance 17.3 (+6.3, the card items and the gate decision). This is the manipulation, not an outcome: it measures how much interaction the card inserts, and says nothing about whether that interaction was read.

**fig4_distribution.png**

Figure 4. Every session's task time, by condition, with the mean marked. The spread is across tasks, not across participants: the operator is a script, so a wide spread here is task difficulty, not individual variation.
